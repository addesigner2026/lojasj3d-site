# Design System Master — Loja SJ 3D (Umuarama - PR)

## 1. Visual Direction (Light, Modern & Clean — Orange & Black)
- **Theme Mode**: Light-First High-Clarity E-Commerce (Inspired by Anycubic Official Store & Bambu Lab Store, refined with UI/UX Pro Max standards)
- **Brand Identity**: Loja SJ 3D — Clean White surfaces (`#FFFFFF`), Soft Studio Slate (`#F8FAFC`), Carbon Black (`#0F172A`), and Loja SJ Orange (`#F04E23` / `#EA580C`).

## 2. Color Tokens
- `--bg-page`: `#F8FAFC`
- `--bg-surface`: `#FFFFFF`
- `--bg-subtle`: `#F1F5F9`
- `--bg-dark-accent`: `#0F172A`
- `--text-primary`: `#0F172A` (Contrast > 15:1 on white)
- `--text-secondary`: `#475569` (Contrast > 7:1 on white)
- `--text-muted`: `#64748B` (Contrast > 4.6:1 on white)
- `--brand-orange`: `#F04E23`
- `--brand-orange-hover`: `#D93D14`
- `--brand-orange-soft`: `#FFF1EC`
- `--brand-orange-border`: `#FDBA9C`
- `--border-subtle`: `#E2E8F0`
- `--border-strong`: `#CBD5E1`

## 3. UI/UX Pro Max Rules Enforced
- **Vector-Only Icons**: SVG icons (`stroke-width="2"`, `aria-hidden="true"`) used instead of emojis.
- **Contrast & Legibility**: WCAG AA+ compliant text and interactive controls (`>= 4.5:1`).
- **Focus & Reduced Motion**: Explicit `:focus-visible` ring and `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` support.
